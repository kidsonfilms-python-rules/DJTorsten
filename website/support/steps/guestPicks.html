Guest Picks

What is Guest Picks? This is a pre-programmed scene where Guests can request Songs to be played

How to Enable!

Step 1.1: Go to the Scene Settings Page

Navigate to the Scene Settings Page by using the Side Navigation Bar

Step 1.2: Enable Guest Picks

Click the Guest Picks Toggle located under General

Step 1.3: Edit Settings!

Edit any of the Settings. If you need a run-down of what the settings do, Click Here

How to Use!

Step 2.1: Go to the General Page

Navigate to the General Page by using the Side Navigation Bar

Step 2.2: Run the Guest Picks Scene

Click USE which is under the Guest Picks Scene

Now you can add songs and remove songs before you start this scene.

Step 2.3: Start the Guest Picks Scene

Hit START to Start the Guest Picks Scene. Hit STOP or PAUSE to do what they say they do.

FAQ

How do I delete a song? Hover over a song and hit the delete button or right click a song for more options

How do I add songs as a DJ? Click the add songs button which is right above the songs queue and enter the links the the songs you want to add, currently we only support YouTube or YouTube Music Links.
