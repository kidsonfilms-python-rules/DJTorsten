How do I Connect My External Display?

Note

What is an External Display? An external display may be a TV or a Monitor and is used to display Party Stats such as the Queue and Current Song

Step 1: Connect your Display to your Computer

Connect your Display to your Computer by using cables such as HDMI, Thunderbolt, USB Type C, or any other cables

Below are the footprints of each to differentiate them:

Step 2: Go to the Scene Settings Page

Navigate to the Scene Settings Page by using the Side Navigation Bar

Step 3: Connect Display to DJFlame

Toggle the External Display button on and click then click the Connect External Display Button thats pops up. Select your External Display in the window that pops up and You're Done!
