Getting Started

In order to use DJFlame, first download and sign up for DJFlame which is 100% FREE.

Download and Sign Up

  1. Go to the Download Page and click on your Computer OS

  2. Follow the Sign Up instructions

  3. Run the Downloaded Installer and follow its instructions

  4. And You're Done!

How to Set Up

All you have to do is Sign In using the credentials you used to Sign Up in the previous section. Once you Sign In once, it is unlikely you will have to again.

Next, you select the CREATE PARTY Button or Join Last Party button in order to create or join a party.

How to Use

Once you Created or Joined a party, you can start setting up your party!

Step 1: Go to the Scene Settings Page

Hover over the Sidebar (Figure 1) to see the Page Menu (Figure 2)

Click on Scene Settings (Figure 3)

Step 2: Change the Settings

You can change each setting by clicking on it, here is an overview on each of them

Note

When the beta tag is next to a setting, it means the setting may be buggy

Scene means a certain feature of the party

General

General currently has one setting, Guest Picks. Turn it on to enable the Guest Picks Scene.

Guest Picks

Guest Picks has 2 settings and one button

1. Clear Cache

Empties the Downloaded Songs Folder

2. External Display

Turn this on to display the party stats on a TV/Monitor. Click the Toggle, and then click Select Monitor. When the window pops up, select your TV/Monitor.

3. Song Length

Pretty self explantory, select either a set length or a range on how long you want your song to play for.

Step 3: Start your Party!

You can go back to the General page by clicking it on the Navigator Bar. When you are ready, click USE on one of the scenes and then click START
