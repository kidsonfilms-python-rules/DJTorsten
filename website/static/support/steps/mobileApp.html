DJFlame Mobile App

How do you Use the Mobile App?

Step 1: Download and Open the App

Download the App From the Play Store or the App Store. When the App is done installing, open it.

Step 2: Fill out the Info

Type in the Party Code and a Username. Username Inputs could also be left empty and it will generate a username for you. You can edit usernames later.

Step 3: And You're Done!

All Things to do with this app from now on are self-explantory and will include instructions if it is unclear.
